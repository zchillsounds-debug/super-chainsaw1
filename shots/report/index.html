The short version

Almost every problem lives in shared code. All 18 characters use one skeleton, one head, one torso and one set of animation clips, so a fix in human.js or anim.js lands on everyone at once. That is good news for effort. It is also why NPCs and enemies barely look like different people: they share one face and differ only in colours, beards and hats.

Proposed fix order

Each step ends with before and after shots plus a draw-call check

  1. Quick bug fixes

    Veils that let the chin and nose through, the bow hanging behind the back, the flat torch flame, the shield floating off the arm, the spear passing through the helmet, the dark slit in robe fronts, crowd-tier eyes buried in the head, and cloth that flares out after a jump. Small, isolated changes that improve every screen.

    code · while you start on Gemini

  2. Faces and heads

    Rebuild the head: skull, brow ridge, chin and jaw, a thicker neck, smaller eyes set into sockets with relaxed lids, tapered eyebrows on the skin, a closed resting mouth, and beards that grow from the face. Then add face settings (age, nose, jaw, cheeks, brow) so Ishaq, Yusuf, Bishr, 'Amr, Rafi', Farud, Hisham and Ghassan look like different men. Rework turbans and the qalansuwa so they read as wound cloth and tall felt, not soft caps.

    code · guided by Gemini refs 13–18

  3. Bodies

    A male torso: remove the chest bulges, flatter pecs, a wider waist, trapezius and shoulders. Let bellies show through robes. Real builds per role (heavy smith, plump merchant, wiry 'ayyar, stooped elder). Boots with soles and heels.

    code

  4. Clothing

    Wire in your cloth, leather, felt and mail textures and the trim bands. Layered garments (qaba over a shirt, robes hitched into belts for fighters), sleeve folds, a cloak that hangs from the shoulders, and distinct gear for the captains.

    code + Gemini textures 1–12

  5. Animation

    Proper strafing steps, idle weight shifts and personality (elder stoop, merchant sway), weapon-specific attacks for dagger, torch, spear and bow (with a real draw), directional hit reactions, and deaths that land on the ground, including Jabir's fall in the prologue.

    code

Rules for every texture

  • Seamless and tileable, square 1024×1024 PNG. Tile it 2×2 to check; any visible seam means regenerate.
  • Add to the prompt: "flat even lighting, no shadows, no highlights, no vignette, straight top-down orthographic view, fills the whole frame, no objects, no text, no watermark".
  • Check before uploading: no crescents, crosses, stars (8-pointed included), domes or minarets, and no letters or script, even decorative. Leave the word "Islamic" out of prompts; it invites all of these.
